# Reference study

Reviewed 9 November 2026. These are bounded observations about communication, not endorsements of every project claim or a ranking of designers. No reference's project data or reported impact was independently audited.

This records the first review. The subsequent expanded review is documented in [the coverage ledger](coverage-ledger.md), [deep analysis](deep-reference-analysis.md), and [writing/composition analysis](writing-and-composition.md). Those files supersede the initial scope statements below where coverage expanded, including Student Progress, additional Neha/Sukhman cases and the curated directory.

## Inspection scope

Read the two requested portfolio homepages and representative public cases. Read Anuj's deployed homepage, Meditimer, Himalayan 450 and My Alumnus, plus local project data and standing briefs. Additional references were inspected where accessible.

Rendered pages were inspected with Chromium using verified curl fetches for HTTPS resources because Chromium did not trust the environment proxy certificate. TLS verification remained enabled in curl. A proposed persistent browser trust-store change was rejected by automatic approval review and was not made. This is a sampled content/composition review, not a complete interaction, accessibility, network, or user study. Some media is lazy-loaded; individual video frames and every disclosure state were not reviewed. Do not infer missing assets from a blank region in a capture.

## The two requested sites

### Sukhman: a close structural reference

Sources: [portfolio](https://portfolio-website-sukh0402.vercel.app/), [projects](https://portfolio-website-sukh0402.vercel.app/projects), [Drive Wise](https://portfolio-website-sukh0402.vercel.app/projects/drive-wise), [Drive Wise deep dive](https://portfolio-website-sukh0402.vercel.app/projects/drive-wise/full), [My Alumnus](https://portfolio-website-sukh0402.vercel.app/projects/my-alumnus).

The homepage separates projects and research. Its project summaries try to explain the product and task rather than only name a discipline. Drive Wise combines a product visual, brief, role, duration, five-stage index, research material, concept logic, and selected interface moments. The highlights/deep-dive split creates two levels of detail.

Particularly transferable: its owner interview counterexample, distinction between concept logic and a functioning model, and separation of synthetic scenarios, informal feedback, planned fixes, and target KPIs. The reader can inspect a progression from owner uncertainty to an explanatory interface.

Use selectively. Willingness to pay in a survey does not validate the designed product. A strong summary headline can overstate the qualifications in the body. The gap-analysis conclusion needs defined comparison criteria; derived scores and correlations need their methods. Do not reproduce repeated thesis slogans, tiny labels, section counts, or every artifact merely because they create density.

### My Alumnus: shared-project attribution needs care

The current public case provides research, role-specific views, a walkthrough, implementation claims, and different forms of testing. It is useful for studying how a workflow becomes a product explanation. Anuj's case describes his collaboration and attributes findings to a supplied report; the collaborator's current opening broadly attributes research and decisions to its designer. This is a discrepancy to clarify, not evidence that either person's credit should automatically be overwritten. The live app may also have advanced beyond Anuj's October captures. Keep versions, personal contribution and shared results explicit.

### Neha Saraswat: consequential choices and system context

Sources: [portfolio](https://nehasaraswat.in/PORTFOLIO.html), [Walmart assortment](https://nehasaraswat.in/walmart-assortment-case-study-preview.html), [[247].ai system view](https://nehasaraswat.in/247ai-systems-case-study.html), [Jifflenow](https://nehasaraswat.in/jifflenow-case-study-preview.html).

The homepage establishes leadership scope and offers recognizable project summaries. The open system-view case gives a route through several connected problems, roles and interfaces. Its account links understanding roles and taxonomy to handoff design and shared product structure. Jifflenow explains an information-model choice and connects it to the meeting-booking interface; it includes alternatives and reflection. These are useful patterns for making a consequential choice inspectable.

Anuj should borrow the specific constraint, reason, interface example and contribution, at the scale of his actual work. Executive positioning and organization-wide impact are inappropriate substitutes for project evidence.

Treat numbers critically. The Walmart homepage uses a days-based time reduction while its case detail gives a weeks-based reduction. These may concern different measures, but the scope is not reconciled in the inspected text. Do not reproduce the figures as independently verified. Some cases are marked protected; only public material was reviewed, with no access controls bypassed. Captions and decorative devices on this site do not override Anuj's rules.

## The selected principles

| Need | Useful reference pattern | Adaptation for Anuj |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish the project quickly | Sukhman's product-specific summaries; Drew's compact opening | Retain a concise task, actual output and contribution on each case page |
| Explain judgment | Neha's information-model choices; Drew's constrained workflow decisions | Tie an actual constraint and supported reason to the screen or interaction |
| Show learning | Elizabeth's tested hypotheses; Bettina's unexpected observation | Include a genuine change in understanding and its design consequence |
| Support depth | Moritz's accessible artifacts; Sukhman's layered accounts | Select evidence and link additional source material where useful |
| Make findings actionable | Nicole's finding/action pairs | Use connected prose that says what changed and why |
| Let readers scan | Stage index, early summary, optional explorations | Keep the existing vertical navigation and essential content visible |
| Remain credible | Qualified research, actual status, measured/projected distinction | Preserve sample, ownership, version and evidence limits |

The resulting skill is a synthesis of these patterns and Anuj's explicit requirements. It is not a copied layout, a universal research process, or evidence that a project followed every stage historically.
