Explanations of their effects are editorial interpretations, not access to the authors' private intentions or proof of reader performance. Measurements describe this capture environment only.

## Sukhman: two levels of explanation

The project index leads into cases with a product description, metadata, brief and problem. Its main pages use repeated horizontal rules, compact headings, grouped numbers and selected artifacts. Drive Wise also has a substantially more expansive full case. The relation between the short and full versions is more useful than either length on its own: one establishes the argument, while the other exposes the supporting material.

In the captured desktop DOM, BINA, MyJio and SDG opening headings and introductory text commonly resolve to 15px with an 18px line height. Their upper-level differentiation therefore depends heavily on weight, case, spacing, rules and layout rather than a large type hierarchy. At 390px, Drive Wise keeps its compact hierarchy and several metadata columns. The document fit the viewport, but fitting is not the same as comfortable reading. Anuj should retain his established type tokens and give substantial prose sufficient line height.

### Sentence and paragraph construction

The stronger passages name a situation, a specific finding or constraint, and a consequence. For example, the bin's placement account explains how sensor position affects what can be detected; the lid incident explains why an apparently removable feature mattered to initial acknowledgment. Those connections carry more information than announcing a research method.

The weaker tendency is repeating a thesis sentence between sections, or making a short conclusion stronger than its longer account. When adapting this structure, assign each section a different question. A research paragraph establishes evidence; the next paragraph interprets it; a design paragraph describes the resulting behavior. Do not repeat a broad claim about clarity at all three levels.

The deep Drive Wise report and main account also differ on the questionnaire item count. This could reflect scope or version, but it is not resolved by reading the number that appears most often. Treat inconsistencies as a source-reconciliation task. Anuj's consumer report and notebook need the same discipline.

### Writing mechanism

The useful decision passages identify the entity or relationship being changed, explain the alternative, then trace the consequence into workflow or product structure. This is more precise than an assertion that a system became intuitive. Anuj can use that mechanism at the scale of a single dose, visit, task or ticket.

Some summary language claims broad transformation or organizational impact. Such language depends on role, contribution, measurement and timescale. It should not be borrowed merely because it sounds confident. The assortment page's large financial figures and differently scoped cycle-time claims especially need source and attribution; the review does not certify them.

### Interaction and narrow-screen observations

The system-view page exposes four foldout buttons. In the inspected capture, clicking them did not change the body's text and no `aria-expanded` value was reported. This does not establish a reliable accessible disclosure, nor does it prove the native live page is broken: transport and initial state may affect the result. Treat the visual presence of a disclosure separately from exercised behavior.

At a 390px viewport the captured system-view document measured 412px wide. This is a specific reproduction under the review setup, not an exhaustive responsive audit. It is enough to reject copying its desktop composition without a phone check.

## Applying intentionality without inventing intent

For any proposed element, finish the sentence: “The reader needs to assess ___, so this element shows ___ using ___.” Then identify its cost and the simpler available alternative. If the sentence cannot be completed with actual project material, the element has not earned its place.

For any proposed paragraph, identify its job and its source. For any asserted reason, distinguish the author's confirmed rationale from a present editorial interpretation. For any result, identify how it was observed. This makes the resulting portfolio deliberate without pretending that every reference detail is successful, purposeful or appropriate to Anuj.
