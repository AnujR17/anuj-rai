# Serene case-study writing guide

This guide applies to `portfolioSerene`. Improve the existing five cases and their original artifacts; do not replace this branch with the older portfolio layout or merge the branches.

## The essential story

Open with the specific question, the work, your contribution and its status. For each consequential choice, name the evidence, explain the visible design response and identify its tradeoff. End with what was made and the next useful evaluation question. Original project artifacts, newly reconstructed browser studies and tested results are different kinds of evidence; make their relationship clear.

The current structure is:

1. Project question, short introduction, contribution, status and original-project link.
2. Original artifact in its contextual mockup, with a source caption.
3. The situation and the tension the work addresses.
4. Evidence, choices and tradeoffs, with an explanatory visual.
5. A separately labelled interaction study: what to try and what to notice.
6. What was made, what to evaluate next, and optional source/credit detail.

## Add depth when the author can confirm it

Original alternatives, rejected directions, personal rationale and feedback make a case more distinctive. Add the actual artifact and account when supplied. Do not invent an iteration because a portfolio convention expects one. A useful pattern is: evidence → documented choice → tradeoff → actual result → next question.

Use measured outcomes only with the measure, method, sample and appropriate scope. Never invent participant quotes, testing counts, sunlight performance, clinical improvements or focus/productivity gains. Keep the medicine app, physical group concept and survey analysis separate.

## Visuals should explain something

Keep DM Sans, Serene’s paper background and project accents, contextual mockups and existing studies. Every addition should help inspect the work, understand a relationship or evaluate the reasoning. Caption original assets with their source; label new diagrams and browser studies as explanatory. Preserve keyboard operation, responsive reading and essential content without JavaScript.
