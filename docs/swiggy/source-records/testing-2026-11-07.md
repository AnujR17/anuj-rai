## Stage 9 — Testing

**Status:** Heuristic evaluation done 7 Nov 2026. Fixes are proposed and wait for the owner's approval.

**Method:** Heuristic Evaluation #60 only (owner decision, 7 Nov). There was no think-aloud and no Wizard of Oz. Usability Report #117 is below.

### How it was run
- **Evaluator:** one (Claude). The owner chose not to act as a second evaluator. The evaluator also built the prototype, which is a known bias. Both points go to Stage 12.
- **What was tested:** the five tickets in the live prototype (Agent Desk v7.1). Each was played from handover to Ticket closed, along the intended path, with a screenshot at every step:
  - SW-2041 wrong item
  - SW-2070 missing item
  - SW-2063 not delivered
  - SW-2091 quality complaint, cash on delivery
  - SW-2078 late delivery
- **The decision taken on each path was the evaluator's choice.** It exercises the interface; it is not a judgement on the case:

| Ticket | Fault | Action |
|---|---|---|
| SW-2041 | Restaurant | Full refund |
| SW-2070 | Restaurant | Partial refund |
| SW-2063 | — | Send to review |
| SW-2091 | Customer | Deny |
| SW-2078 | Rider | Partial refund + ₹50 coupon |

- **Heuristics:**
  - **Project heuristics** (from the Stage 7 admissibility test and the Stage 6 principles): H1 Objective · H2 Traceable · H3 No prediction · H4 No prescription · H5 Fair to all three parties · H6 Glanceable mid-chat · H7 Nothing left to assumption.
  - **Six of Nielsen's ten usability heuristics:** U1 Visibility of system status · U2 Consistency and standards · U3 Error prevention · U4 Recognition over recall · U5 User control and freedom · U6 Minimalist design.
- **Severity:** Nielsen's 0–4 scale:
  - 0 not a problem
  - 1 cosmetic
  - 2 minor
  - 3 major
  - 4 blocks the decision

### Effort measures (counted from the walkthroughs, not from agents)
| Ticket | Actions to Confirm | Thinking points (M) | Column switches | Places looked up | Estimated expert time to Confirm (KLM) | Clicks to close after Confirm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW-2041 wrong item | 21 | 10 | 9 | 3 | ~41 s | 7 |
| SW-2070 missing item | 20 | 9 | 7 | 3 | ~38 s | 7 |
| SW-2063 not delivered | 17 | 7 | 3 | 2 | ~32 s | 7 |
| SW-2091 quality (COD) | 17 | 8 | 7 | 2 | ~33 s | 6 |
| SW-2078 late delivery | 21 | 10 | 7 | 3 | ~42 s | 8 |

**How to read the measures:**
- **Actions:** clicks plus typed entries, counted from opening the chat to Confirm.
- **KLM (Keystroke-Level Model) estimate:**
  - Each action: point and click, 1.3 s.
  - Each digit typed: 0.28 s, plus 0.4 s each way moving the hand between mouse and keyboard.
  - Each decision point: 1.35 s.
- **What KLM leaves out:** reading time and waiting for the simulated customer, restaurant or rider, so real handling time is longer. It is an estimate for comparing tickets and versions, not a measured time.
- **Pattern:**
  - **Most actions:** about half of them are the agent sending scripted chat lines.
  - **Most effort:** the two tickets that need a party contact plus evidence review (SW-2041, SW-2078).
  - **Fewest column switches:** SW-2063, because it has no photo to review.

### Never-shown check
**Passed in all five tickets.** None of these appeared at any step:
- a recommended outcome or pre-selected action
- a suggested amount
- a score of any kind
- a "verified" or "suspicious" label
- a predicted review outcome

The decision draft is generated from the action the agent chose, so it doesn't prescribe (H4 pass).

### Findings (Usability Report #117)
Ranked by severity. **No severity-4 findings:** every ticket could be decided and closed.

| ID | Sev | Heuristic | Where | Finding | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | 3 | U5, U3 | Decision · Partial | A partial refund is always the full price of the affected items. The agent can't refund part of an item, such as the missing vadas in SW-2070's ₹220 combo. In SW-2078, "partial" refunded all items (₹420) | Add an amount field for partial refunds, capped at the affected items' price, empty by default (no suggested amount) |
| F2 | 3 | U3, H6 | Reply box | The decision draft is longer than the one-line reply box, so the agent can send it without seeing the whole message | Let the reply box grow to 3–4 lines while a draft is loaded |
| F3 | 3 | H7, H6 | Policy checks | The checks start collapsed, so failing or unknown conditions and triggers sit behind a click (for example T4 "Conflicting records" in SW-2063, and P4 COD timing not met in SW-2091). Collapsed, only the stacked icons show | Open by default, or always show rows that are not met or unknown |
| F4 | 2 | H7 | Policy checks | Conditions in the frozen policy are missing from the screen: P3 claim window, P8 approval limit, and trigger T1 high value. SW-2063 is ₹721, over the ₹500 limit, yet T1 doesn't show | Add P3 and T1 from the frozen set, or record the cut in Stage 11 |
| F5 | 2 | H1, H2 | Queue rail | The red priority dash has no stated rule (SW-2063 and SW-2078 are set by hand) and no reason on hover. It conflicts with the Stage 8 rule "no priority score" | Define it (for example "waited over N min") and show the reason on hover, or remove it |
| F6 | 2 | H1, H5 | Rider and restaurant name bar | Star ratings are coloured red, amber or green. The colour reads as a verdict on that party, and the customer has no rating, so the parties aren't treated the same | Show the rating in neutral ink |
| F7 | 2 | H2 | AI Summary | The summary of the bot conversation can't be traced: the bot transcript it comes from can't be opened | Click to open the bot transcript |
| F8 | 2 | H5, U2 | History summary | The counts are written in different orders: customer "Raised/Refunded" (total first), rider "Liable/Complaints" and restaurant "Upheld/Complaints" (outcome first). No time window is shown | One order for all three parties (outcome / total) and a "Last 90 days" caption |
| F9 | 2 | H6 | Customer evidence (SW-2041) | The photo was sent 40 min after the complaint, but no glanceable fact says so. The viewer shows only minutes after delivery | Add "sent N min after complaint" in the viewer and on the evidence row |
| F10 | 2 | H7 | Decision area | Who pays shows only after an action is picked. During the investigation the restaurant's cost in SW-2091 isn't visible | Show the default liable party (P2) next to Issue type from the start |
| F11 | 2 | H5, H6 | SW-2063 | The customer's claim and the rider's record sit in different tabs. The side-by-side conflict view (M4.2) wasn't built | Add a two-line conflict block in the insights column when T4 fires |
| F12 | 2 | H6, U4 | Order details (SW-2078) | "Delivered 35 min after promised" isn't stated anywhere. The agent has to add up the step delays in the Rider tab | Add a "Delivered N min after promised" line |
| F13 | 1 | H2, H1 | Rider Trip log | Step delays (+N) are coloured red or green against planned times that are Hypothetical and not shown | Show the planned time on hover; neutral colour |
| F14 | 1 | U2 | Cost line | Shows an internal label: "v1.3 mapping, Inferred" | Plain wording, for example "per working policy (Hypothetical)" |
| F15 | 1 | U6 | Interaction history vs History summary | The same counts appear in both columns | Keep the counts in one place |
| F16 | 1 | H7 | Customer evidence (SW-2063) | With no photo, the row greys out and says "No photo" only in a tooltip | Write "No photo" on the row |

**Headline:** the screen keeps its main promise: facts and conditions, no verdicts. The biggest problems are hidden facts (F3, F4, F10, F11, F12) and the partial refund (F1).

### What each ticket showed
| Ticket | Main check (from the plan) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| SW-2041 | Is the 40-min gap noticed? Does history read as background? | Gap not glanceable (F9). History reads neutrally, but its counts are in mixed orders (F8) |
| SW-2070 | The shortest path; does "no default outcome" add friction? | No friction from the unselected actions: 20 actions, ~38 s estimated. Partial can't fit a part-item refund (F1) |
| SW-2063 | Are both sides visible at once? | No: tabs, plus the trigger behind a collapsed panel (F3, F11). Missing T1 (F4) |
| SW-2091 | Is the restaurant's cost visible? Same treatment of restaurant history? | History treated the same. Cost hidden until an action is chosen (F10) |
| SW-2078 | Is the delay clear and objective? | Delay split across steps (F12); colour coding and planned times not sourced (F13) |
