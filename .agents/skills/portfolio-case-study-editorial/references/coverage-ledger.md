# Source coverage ledger

Reviewed 9 November 2026. This ledger makes the boundaries of the review explicit. It does not certify project outcomes, datasets or private author intent.

## How to interpret the coverage

- **Text and capture:** page text and structure were collected for editorial review, with a full-page rendered capture available. Visual analysis used selected views and crops; this label does not mean every pixel, video frame or interaction was inspected.
- **Index:** reviewed for project discovery and positioning. A listed project is not automatically a reviewed case.
- **Restricted:** requested content could not be inspected. No equivalent findings are inferred from its reputation.
- **Anuj deployed sample:** three public cases and the homepage were inspected. The applied plan for the remaining cases uses current repository data and briefs; it is not a claim of fresh live testing of all routes.

The primary-site inventory covers all five internally hosted project cases listed on Sukhman’s Work index, both available full cases examined here, three linked research reports, and all nine public case pages linked on Neha’s portfolio. Sukhman’s three relevant Behance links remain inaccessible. This is bounded coverage of those indexes at the review date, not a claim that the designers have no other work.

## Rendering and behavior checks

Rendered with Chromium at 1440×1000 and selected pages at 390×844, with reduced motion. Chromium could not use the environment proxy certificate directly, so HTTPS resources were fulfilled from normally verified curl requests. No TLS bypass or persistent trust change was made. This changes the transport and may affect some assets, fonts, headers or interactions; the review is not a native cross-browser fidelity certification.
