## Project-specific evidence guardrails

These notes summarize inspected briefs and dated context. They are not permission to add every listed claim to the site.

- **Meditimer:** independent app, physical group product and medicine survey remain separate. The survey's 52 responses include overlapping paths; 48 caregiver and seven senior paths include three people in both. Denominators vary by question. Do not silently present them as 55 distinct respondents or direct senior research for all answers.
- **Physical Meditimer:** preserve individual concept/function/interaction and field-research contributions versus teammate mockup/documentation work. A model does not prove dispensing, sensing or electronics. Confirm fabrication details before expanding credit.
- **Himalayan:** mixed two-wheeler survey context does not establish Himalayan-only evaluation. Browser behavior is not safe riding, sunlight legibility or reduced glance time. Preserve original and assisted implementation credit.
- **Toki:** current briefs supersede older connection-only descriptions. Firmware/app versions and demonstrated behavior must be checked again before publication. Compilation is not physical acceptance or improved focus.
- **India in Orbit:** show implemented navigation, information grouping and representations. Distinguish illustrative simulation from propagated data; avoid unmeasured learning outcomes and invented audience research.
- **Roundabouts:** 298 record rows are not automatically unique accidents or exposure-adjusted risk. The reported poster feedback is a qualitative pilot; do not turn it into a randomized A/B result or reduced crashes/congestion.
- **My Alumnus:** supplied report, fictional Sample University screens, collaborator ownership and version are material. One guard completing four tasks with one failed save is a specific finding, not broad operational validation.
- **Swiggy:** a developing policy/scenario study. A composition cover is not an original product screenshot; agent research, implemented operations and performance need evidence.
- **Consumer Experience:** supplied dataset analysis is not personal recruitment; saved report/notebook inconsistencies need reconciliation. Associations do not establish causal sales effects.
- **Skidge:** constructed labels/scores, possible leakage and altered statistical output need methodological review before promoting metrics. Do not describe model fit as independently validated employability.
